ReDot's frontend uses dynamically linked Qt 6.8.3 libraries.
Qt Core/Gui/Widgets/Network/QML/Quick/Quick Controls/Quick Effects/Multimedia are used
under GNU LGPL version 3. Qt and its third-party components retain their
respective copyrights and licenses. Qt is copyright The Qt Company and
other contributors. Complete matching source archives and the license texts
are provided in this distribution's licenses/Qt/sources and notices folders.

You may replace the Qt DLLs and plugins with compatible rebuilt versions.
ReDot does not verify or lock their signatures, and does not prohibit
reverse engineering for debugging modifications to these libraries. Rebuild
instructions are contained in the supplied Qt sources. Use MSVC 2022 x64
and the same Qt module/version/ABI; put replacement DLLs alongside ReDot
and matching plugins/QML modules in their corresponding deployment folders.
Qt6Multimedia.dll (Qt6Multimediad.dll in Debug) has a small ReDot playback
repair in src/multimedia/windows/qwindowsaudiosink.cpp: round timer waits up
and recheck WASAPI padding before stopping the output client. It also has
opt-in bounded playback PCM diagnostics. Its private AudioClient header and
the supplied ReDot trace header support these changes. An unsuccessful
WinMM audio-output trial was removed from production; it is not a claimed
distortion repair. All other Qt
libraries and the FFmpeg/QML plugins remain unmodified. The exact change and
build recipe are included in licenses/Qt/changes/qt-audio-padding.patch and
build_qt_audio.py and qt_playback_trace.h; pristine corresponding Qt sources remain in
licenses/Qt/sources. The project README, docs/player-output.md and historical docs/playback-audio.md explain
the build. The script applies the changes to the matching archive in a
project-local build folder, using MSVC 2022 x64, CMake and Ninja Multi-Config.
For full-source rebuilding, apply the patch to qtmultimedia 6.8.3 and build
the Multimedia target against matching Qt 6.8.3. Public/plugin ABI is unchanged.
Qt Multimedia and its dynamically linked FFmpeg
7.1 backend are used only for media playback; recording remains ReDot's
own WGC/D3D11/NVENC/WASAPI/Media Foundation pipeline. FFmpeg is LGPL version
2.1 or later, built without GPL/nonfree features or command-line programs.
Matching upstream source, license texts and the observed build configuration
are supplied alongside the Qt sources. Compatible FFmpeg DLL replacement
and reverse engineering to debug library modifications are permitted too.
The project-local aqt 3.3.0 installer has a
documented UTF-8/Windows ANSI decoding fix; it is a development tool, not
an application runtime library. GPL-licensed development utilities are not
linked into ReDot. Shader tooling is used only during development.
